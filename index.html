How TuneWave works

01

Enter a song, artist, genre, or mood. TuneWave saves recent search terms on this device.

02

Choose your app

On Android, send the search to NewPipe. On PC or iPhone/iPad, open the YouTube results page.

03

NewPipe controls audio and background playback on Android. TuneWave does not extract or host YouTube audio.

⌘

A student-built music-search bridge

TuneWave's interface has no ad placements and no paid membership. NewPipe is a separate, community-built Android app, not an official YouTube app. It uses YouTube's web/internal interfaces, which can change; unavailable, restricted, or removed videos may not play. On PC/iOS, this website opens regular YouTube, so ad-free background playback is not guaranteed there.
